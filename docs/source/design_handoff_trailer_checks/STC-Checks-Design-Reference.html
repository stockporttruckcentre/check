DESIGN SYSTEM · UX SPECIFICATION

Trailer checks that beat the clipboard

The design system for the STC yard app that replaces the paper vehicle check sheet. It covers the components, their states, how they behave, and full worked flows for yard staff doing a check out or a check in on a phone.

00

Principles

Each decision in this system was checked against one question: is this quicker, clearer and more dependable than a clipboard and pen? Where the answer was no, it was redesigned.

01
One job per screen
Each screen asks for one thing: a trailer number, a photo, a tyre reading. The big button at the bottom always does the next thing.
02
You can always see what’s left
The check list shows every step with a tick, an empty ring or a red mark. Nothing is hidden behind a menu.
03
It saves as you go
Every tap and photo is saved to the phone straight away. Signal is only needed to send. Closing the app loses nothing.
04
Can’t send it half done
Send only works when every required photo and check is done. If something is missing, the app lists it and takes you to it.
05
Photos first, typing last
Most of the record is photos and taps. Typing is limited to the trailer number, a few readings and short notes, with quick phrases offered.
06
Built for the yard
Buttons are at least 56px tall, the main button is 64px, text never drops below 16px, and everything works with gloves in bright sun.
07
Shape and colour together
Every status has its own shape (tick, ring, triangle, clock), so nothing depends on seeing colour.
08
Same words as the paper sheet
Labels match the sheet the yard already uses: Check out, Check in, Ministry plate, Landing gear, Internal straps.
Inspection model
We compared three ways of structuring an inspection and chose the hybrid. The rest of the system is built around it.
Long form
One scrolling page of fields, just like the paper sheet.
Easy to lose your place, and on a phone you can’t see what’s missing.
Strict wizard
One step at a time, in a fixed order.
Yards don’t work in a fixed order. If it starts raining you want the photos done first.
Checklist hub + guided steps
A home list of every step, shown with ticks. Next walks through them in order, and you can tap any step to do it out of order.
Shows exactly what’s left like a wizard, without forcing the order. Chosen.

Rules
  • Button labels are verbs plus the thing: "Take photo", "Next: Tyres", "Send inspection". Never "OK", "Submit" or "Continue".
  • When something is missing, the main button reads "3 things left" and opens the missing list, so it is never dead.
  • After any tap that saves or sends, the button locks for 600ms to stop double taps.
  • Destructive actions are never the main button and always need confirming in a sheet.

Gallery and retake
Tap a thumbnail to see it big. Retake keeps its place in the list. Delete asks first and can be undone for 6 seconds.
Behaviour
  • The camera opens on the next missing shot. After "Use photo", the next shot loads with a 300ms slide and a short buzz.
  • Each photo is shrunk to a standard JPEG of about 100KB on the phone the moment it’s taken. Only the small copy is saved and uploaded. See Photo size in section 28.
  • Each photo is stamped with the time, GPS position (if allowed), trailer number and shot name, so photos can never get mixed up between trailers.
  • Uploading from the gallery is off by default. If an admin turns it on, those photos are labelled "From gallery" on the record.
  • A photo that fails to save shows a red mark on its thumbnail and an error banner. Nothing fails silently.

Offline and sync

The app is built to work offline first. The phone keeps the full record and the office receives a copy. Losing signal is shown as normal, not as an error.

04
Sent
The card turns green and the badge changes to SENT.

Couldn’t send
Red banner on Home, plus a phone notification if the app is closed.
Rules
  • The stock sheet copy and last check for each trailer are downloaded at the start of each shift and refreshed whenever there’s signal, so a trailer can be found without signal.
  • "Saved" always means saved on this phone. The word "Sent" only appears once the office has confirmed it received the record.
  • Waiting sends retry with back-off: 10 seconds, 30 seconds, then every 2 minutes. They also retry on app open and when signal returns.
  • If a trailer was changed in the office while the phone was offline, the office record wins and the inspector sees what changed.
  • Signing out with unsent work is blocked, with a list of what’s waiting.

Words

Write like a good yard foreman talks: short and plain. Use the same words as the paper sheet, and always say what to do next.

Principles
  • Use the yard’s words: Check out / Check in, Nearside / Offside, Ministry plate, Landing gear.
  • Lead with the action: "Take photo: Interior", not "Interior photo required".
  • Errors say what happened, then what to do: "Photo didn’t save. Your phone is full. Delete old photos and try again."
  • No blame: "2 things left", not "You haven’t completed".
  • Never "Something went wrong" unless there really is no better explanation, and then add "Your work is safe on this phone."
  • British spelling. No exclamation marks, except on the final "Sent".

Accessibility and the yard

Designed for gloves, rain, sun glare, dirty screens, reading glasses left in the cab, and people who don’t use apps much.

Gloves and wet screens
Targets at least 56px, 12px apart. No swipe-only actions. The shutter is 84px. Steppers are used instead of small fields.
Bright sun
Navy on white for all text. Status colours clear 5.5:1. No thin or grey-on-grey text. An optional high-contrast mode in Settings uses black on white with 3px borders.
Reduced vision
The app follows the phone’s text size up to 200%, and layouts stack rather than cut off. Minimum size is 16px.
Colour blindness
Every status pairs colour with a shape and a word. Green and red are never the only difference.
Reduced dexterity
No precise drawing: damage uses zones. Long press is never required. Undo is offered for 6 seconds after deletes.
One hand
Main actions are in the bottom third. The top bar holds only back, close and save status.
Screen readers
Every icon button has a label. Each step change is announced ("Photo 3 of 9, Offside front"), and toasts are announced politely.
New to apps
No hidden gestures, no hamburger menu. Every screen has one main button that says what it does. First use shows a 3-card intro, which can be skipped.
Feedback by touch
Short buzzes back up what’s on screen and are never the only feedback. They can be turned off in Settings.

Rules
  • Pins are numbered in the order they’re added: 1, 2, 3. The same number is used on the report and in the photo file names.
  • Damage already on record shows as grey lettered pins (A, B). Tapping one offers "Still there" or "Repaired", so check-ins show what changed.

  • Removing a pin keeps its photos in the recycle bin for 30 days. Numbers aren’t reused, so pin 3 is always pin 3.

  • Shrinking and converting happens on the phone before upload, so full-size photos never reach storage and uploads work on weak signal.
  • Every photo becomes a .jpg, whatever the phone takes. No WebP or HEIC, so anyone can open them on any PC.
  • Target size is 100KB. The app starts at 1600px and quality 80, then steps quality down until the file is under 100KB. It never goes below quality 55; if it still doesn’t fit, it drops to 1280px. Hard limit 150KB.
  • Photos are turned the right way up before saving, so none arrive sideways.
  • Location and other hidden phone data are removed from the file. The time and place are kept on the check record instead, where they can’t be edited.
  • A blur check runs on the small copy. If it’s too blurry to read, the camera asks for a retake before moving on.
  • Target size, long edge and the 150KB limit are admin settings in System. Changing them only affects new photos.
Rules
  • Files are named the moment a photo is taken, on the phone. Nobody renames anything.
  • The D number on a file is the pin number on the PDF drawing, so "D03" in the zip is pin 3 on the report.
  • For now every check email goes to alexellis@stc-uk.com. Admins can add addresses per site and per check type in Lists and wording.
  • If a check is reopened, the new email, PDF and zip end in _v2, and the email says what changed.
  • Details.csv holds every field on one row, for anyone who wants to paste it into a spreadsheet.
  • Who gets emails, the subject line, the file name pattern and the PDF footer are all admin settings.
